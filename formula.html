Dosing Protocol

One capsule morning,
one evening.

Hormonal Balance is split across two daily capsules — not for convenience, but because timing changes how effectively each ingredient works.

Morning Capsule

With breakfast

Myo-Inositol D-Chiro-Inositol Methylfolate Methylcobalamin

The inositols work best taken with food and support insulin sensitivity through the hours you’re most metabolically active. The activated B vitamins support methylation and energy production from the start of the day, rather than being wasted overnight.

Evening Capsule

With dinner

DIM Vitex Agnus-Castus P5P (B6)

DIM is absorbed more consistently alongside a meal containing fat. Vitex works through the pituitary’s overnight signaling rhythm, and P5P supports the progesterone and prolactin regulation that eases PMS symptoms — both suited to an evening dose.

What To Expect

A timeline built on
how hormones actually shift.

Hormonal pathways recalibrate gradually, over full menstrual cycles — not overnight. Here is the honest timeline our formulation is designed around.

Weeks 1–2
Foundational Repletion

Methylated B vitamins and inositols begin replenishing cellular stores. Most women notice steadier energy before any visible skin or cycle changes — the groundwork is being laid.

Weeks 3–4
Early Shifts

Insulin sensitivity and estrogen clearance begin to improve. Skin texture and oil production often start to shift, and PMS symptoms may feel slightly less intense heading into the next cycle.

Months 2–3
Visible Change

By the second and third full cycles, most women see a meaningful reduction in hormonal breakouts, more regular cycle length, and noticeably calmer PMS — the pathways are working in concert.

Months 3+
Sustained Balance

With continued use, results compound: clearer skin, a predictable cycle, and stable energy become the new baseline rather than an occasional good month.
