Four pathways.

Hormonal acne, PCOS, and cycle irregularity rarely have a single cause. They emerge from four interconnected pathways — estrogen, androgens, insulin, and cycle signaling — each influencing the others. ENVI is formulated to address all four at once.

The Four Pathways

How ENVI works,
pathway by pathway.

Pathway 01

Estrogen Metabolism

The liver clears estrogen through a two-phase detoxification process. When these pathways are sluggish — from nutrient depletion, poor liver function, or common genetic variants in methylation genes — estrogen isn’t broken down and cleared efficiently. The result is estrogen dominance: estrogen unbalanced relative to progesterone. This is a leading driver of hormonal acne along the jawline, breast tenderness, mood fluctuations, and heavier or irregular periods.

DIM (Diindolylmethane)
Supports healthy estrogen metabolism by promoting a more favorable balance of estrogen metabolites, helping the liver clear excess estrogen rather than recirculate it.
Methylfolate & Methylcobalamin
Active, body-ready forms of B9 and B12 that fuel methylation — the biochemical process the liver depends on to safely deactivate and clear estrogen, especially important for women with reduced methylation capacity.
Pathway 02

Androgen Balance

Androgens are present in every woman’s body, but become disruptive in excess. Elevated androgens over-stimulate the sebaceous glands, increasing oil production and creating the plugged, inflamed pore environment where acne-causing bacteria thrive. This is the mechanism behind persistent jawline and chin breakouts, adult-onset acne, and excess facial or body hair.

DIM
Helps modulate androgen metabolism, supporting a healthier balance between estrogen and androgen activity at the tissue level.
Myo-Inositol
Improves cellular insulin sensitivity, which in turn reduces the ovarian signal that drives excess androgen production — addressing the root cause rather than the downstream symptom.
Pathway 03

Insulin Signaling

When cells become resistant to insulin, the body compensates by producing more of it. Excess insulin directly stimulates the ovaries to produce more androgens — one of the primary mechanisms behind PCOS-related acne, irregular cycles, and stubborn weight gain. Improving insulin sensitivity at the cellular level is one of the most effective ways to reduce androgen excess at its source, rather than managing its downstream effects.

Myo-Inositol & D-Chiro-Inositol (40:1)
This specific 40:1 ratio mirrors the natural balance found in healthy ovarian tissue and is the ratio most studied in clinical research for improving insulin sensitivity and ovulatory function. Mismatched ratios — common in lower-cost 1:1 blends — can actually impair egg quality and worsen insulin signaling.
Pathway 04

Cycle Regulation

Progesterone is the calming counterbalance to estrogen, rising in the second half of the cycle to stabilize mood and prepare the body for menstruation. When pituitary signaling to the ovaries is erratic or progesterone production is insufficient, the result is a shortened luteal phase, PMS, mid-cycle spotting, and unpredictable cycle length.

Vitex Agnus-Castus (Chasteberry)
Supports healthy pituitary signaling to encourage the body’s own natural progesterone production and promote a more regular menstrual cycle.
P5P (Activated Vitamin B6)
The bioactive form of B6, supporting progesterone synthesis and healthy prolactin regulation — easing PMS symptoms like mood swings and breast tenderness.
