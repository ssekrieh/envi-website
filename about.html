Our Story

Born from a decade
of breakouts.

For years, our founder tried everything the skincare aisle had to offer — cleansers, retinoids, prescription creams, dermatologist visits that treated the symptom and never the cause. The breakouts always came back, cyclical and stubborn, worse around the same two weeks of every month.

It wasn’t until a hormone panel finally told the truth: this was never a skin problem. It was a hormone problem, writing itself across her skin in a language the skincare industry had never learned to read. Estrogen dominance. Androgen excess. Insulin resistance. Three invisible imbalances, one very visible result.

So she stopped chasing her complexion and started addressing its cause — and built the supplement brand she wished had existed all along.

The Vision

A clinical wellness brand, built for the vanity.

ENVI exists at the intersection of the clinic and the vanity — rigorous enough to earn a practitioner’s trust, beautiful enough to earn a place in a woman’s daily ritual.

Our founder’s vision was never simply to create another supplement. It was to build the first luxury clinical wellness brand designed entirely around women’s hormonal health — one that refuses to choose between science and beauty, credibility and desirability.

Every formulation decision starts with the research. Every design decision honors the woman taking it. ENVI is meant to sit as comfortably in a dermatology clinic as it does on a bathroom shelf — a daily reminder that treating the cause can still feel like self-care.
