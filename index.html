The Science

Four pathways.
One complete formula.

Hormonal imbalance rarely has a single cause. The pathways governing estrogen, androgens, insulin, and cycle regulation are deeply interconnected. Addressing one without the others delivers partial results at best. ENVI targets all four simultaneously — with the smallest number of ingredients that achieve comprehensive hormonal support.

01
Estrogen Metabolism

Poor estrogen detoxification leads to estrogen dominance — the driver behind hormonal acne, mood fluctuations, and cycle irregularity. Supporting the liver’s processing pathways helps restore healthy hormonal ratios.

DIM · Methylfolate · Methylcobalamin
02
Androgen Balance

Elevated androgens stimulate the sebaceous glands, increasing oil production and driving hormonal breakouts. Restoring androgen balance is one of the most direct routes to clearer, calmer skin.

DIM · Myo-Inositol
03
Insulin Signaling

Insulin resistance elevates androgen production in the ovaries — a primary driver of hormonal acne and PCOS. Improving insulin sensitivity at the cellular level reduces androgen excess from the source.

Myo-Inositol · D-Chiro-Inositol (40:1)
04
Cycle Regulation

Progesterone deficiency and erratic pituitary signaling cause cycle irregularity, PMS, and mid-cycle hormonal surges. Restoring pituitary signaling supports more regular, balanced cycles.

Vitex Agnus-Castus · P5P (B6)
